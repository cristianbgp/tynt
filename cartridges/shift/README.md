# SHIFT

SHIFT is a tiny three-lane reflex game. Stay in the single open lane as pairs of blocks slide in from the right. Each barrier you pass earns one point; a collision ends the run.

**Controls:** Up or A moves one lane up · Down or B moves one lane down · any of those buttons restarts after a collision. Holding a button does not repeat movement.

The player starts in the middle lane. Barriers get faster at scores 5 and 10, then stay at that speed. The **BEST** score lasts for retries during the current run, but tynt does not currently offer persistent cartridge storage, so it resets when the cartridge is reopened.

The monochrome playfield uses tynt's fixed 160 × 144 canvas and four-shade palette. SHIFT has no permanent control legend inside the game screen; the controls are shown on its gallery page and in the emulator controls.

This cartridge is maintained in the tynt public gallery.
