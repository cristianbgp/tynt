# reaction

Wait for the circle to say **GO**, then press A or B as quickly as you can. Pressing early loses the round.

**Controls:** A or B reacts · A or B starts the next round

This cartridge demonstrates deterministic `seed()` and `random()`, global `frame()` timing, `after()` thresholds, repeating `every()` animation, and synthesized feedback.

This cartridge is maintained in the tynt public gallery.
