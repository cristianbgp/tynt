# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

tynt is committed to a welcoming, safe, and constructive community for everyone, regardless of experience, identity, background, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and specific when giving feedback.
- Assume good intent while taking responsibility for impact.
- Keep discussions focused on improving tynt and its cartridges.
- Credit other people's work and respect licensing requirements.
- Accept moderation decisions and corrections gracefully.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, and publishing another person's private information are not acceptable.

## Scope

This code applies in the repository, issue tracker, pull requests, reviews, and other spaces where someone represents the tynt community.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to [@cristianbgp](https://github.com/cristianbgp). Do not include private or sensitive information in a public issue. Reports will be reviewed as promptly and confidentially as possible.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may warn, temporarily restrict, or permanently ban participants whose behavior is inappropriate, threatening, or harmful. Enforcement decisions will consider context, impact, and whether harmful behavior is repeated.
